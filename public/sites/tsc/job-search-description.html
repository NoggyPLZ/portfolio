Job Description

Requisition #: 5000086591606
Job Title: Store Team Member-2513
Category: Retail
City: Owensboro West
State: KY

Job Purpose:

Provide legendary customer service through the use of integrity, product knowledge, positive attitude, and high energy.

Primary Responsibilities:

There are several key responsibilities that a Team Member may be accountable for. These responsibilities will be given directly to them by the Store Manager, Assistant Store Manager or Team Leader and may include the following:
Greet all customer as they enter the store.
Practice 4 Keys to Successful Selling: Welcome the Customer, Make the Sale, Suggest the Sale, Close the Sale.
Provide excellent customer service at the time of the time of the check out with a Sincere ""Thank You"".

Secondary Duties:

Team Members may also be required to perform one or more of the following duties on a regular basis:
Operate cash register/computer
Provide cash control within the department established by Tractor Supply Company
Recovery of merchandise in assigned areas
Mandatory freight as assigned by Store Manager, Assistant Store Manager or Team Leader
Plan-o-grams (merchandising, sets, and resets)
Assembling merchandise
Janitorial
Price changes/markdowns
Deliveries, if 18 years old or older and valid Driver's License
Operate Forklift, if 18 years old or older
Load outs
Other responsibilities as assigned by the Store Manager, Assistant Store Manager or Team Leader
