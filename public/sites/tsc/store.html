A Tractor Supply Store

Our business requires fully-stocked and well-organized stores and our customers expect quick and helpful service from an informative and friendly team. Our Store Team Members are vital to the success of our business.

Store Team Members are upbeat and passionate about providing the best service and making the most out of their careers at Tractor Supply Company. While giving our customers the personal attention they expect, you'll work alongside welders, farmers, and horse owners. Learn our products and business while staying true to our Mission and Values.

Kick off a great career with a Store Team job at Tractor Supply Company.

Day-in-the-Life at Our Stores

career areas






Retail team member

Through their integrity, product knowledge, positive attitude, and high energy, our Store Team Members are the backbone of our legendary customer service. Whether greeting customers, selling products, loading customers’ trucks, driving a forklift, or taking care of baby chicks, our Team Members don’t just go the extra mile – they go the Country Mile.


Receiver

Our Receivers do more than just check in freight – they are key to building an "inventory-aware" culture in their store. With a focus on inventory accuracy, Receivers also focus on feed rotation, and, of course, legendary customer service.


Team Leader

Team Leaders assist Store Management by providing outstanding customer service, promoting a safe and productive work environment, and motivating Team Members. We depend on Team Leaders to set the right example through continuously demonstrating our Mission and Values.


Assistant Store Manager

Our managers are the future of our business. They are the people willing to roll up their sleeves and do what's needed to get the job done. There's no better or faster way to get a feel for our business and understand the customers we serve than with a Store Management position.


Store Manager

Video removed in this demo
TSC Team Members working in a Tractor Supply Company store

If you’re interested in building and leading a terrific team while increasing sales and profits in your store, then you’re the type of manager we want. Our Store Managers sell, stock, merchandise, teach, train, and lead; all while exemplifying our Mission & Values. Don't expect to sit behind a desk; this is a hands-on, sales floor job with a lot of customer interaction that will enable you to make an impact on all aspects of your business.

In addition, our Management Training Program empowers you with the tools and resources you need to hit the ground running and run a successful operation.


District Manager

Opportunities with Tractor Supply don't end at the store level. We're looking for District and Regional Managers as well. In fact, many of our senior executives started out in our stores. Whether you're interested in a Store, District or Regional management position, the possibilities are truly endless.
