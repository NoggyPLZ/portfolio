Ole Red gatlinburg

Ole Red is a country lifestyle brand inspired by Blake Shelton’s clever irreverent third chart hit, "Ol’ Red." Celebrating the laid-back, fun-loving Southern Oklahoma lifestyle, the brand’s first location opened in Shelton’s hometown of Tishomingo, Oklahoma, serving up Shelton-inspired food, drink, music, apparel and home goods. The brand is anchored by a 26,000-square-foot restaurant and entertainment venue in the heart of Music City’s famed Lower Broadway, featuring a two-story bar and restaurant, retail area, state-of-the-art performance space, dance floor, private event space and expansive rooftop. A third location in Gatlinburg, TN opened March 2019.
