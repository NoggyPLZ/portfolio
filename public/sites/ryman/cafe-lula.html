Cafe Lula

A casual Nashville-centric eatery and bar, Café Lula is located outside the front doors of the Ryman Auditorium. The café is open daily featuring local coffee from 8th & Roast, small plates, salads, soups, sandwiches, Nashville’s signature hot chicken, in-house made baked goods and a wide selection of cocktails, beer and wine.
